Maa Yashoda: A Journey of Care, Awareness and Connection

Breast milk is more than nutrition. Through breastfeeding awareness, safe donor human milk and community support, this initiative works to strengthen the Human Milk Bank and help vulnerable newborns receive the care and nourishment they need.

Why It Matters

Awareness is where every act of generosity begins

“A mother's love can travel beyond her own home.”

Many mothers never learn that donor human milk exists, or that their own surplus milk could support a newborn in need. Awareness is the first and quietest part of this work.

Connection is the second. A mother considering donation deserves a conversation rather than a form letter, someone who can explain the process, answer her questions and stay with her through it.

Outreach carries both closer to the communities where mothers actually live, rather than waiting for them to find their way to a hospital. Together these form the foundation of a Human Milk Bank ecosystem that can grow over time.

Human Milk Bank Van

Taking awareness and connection closer to communities

Not every mother who wishes to learn about milk donation can easily travel to the Human Milk Bank. The Van is the outreach component of the initiative, carrying information, guidance and human contact into neighbourhoods, so that distance does not become a barrier to generosity.

  • Community outreachAwareness reaches mothers where they are.
  • Donor guidanceQuestions answered in person, without pressure.
  • ConnectionA clear path between interested mothers and the Human Milk Bank.

Grant Details

A project with a purpose

Rotary Global GrantGG2694832

The initiative has been made possible through collaboration between Rotary organizations, healthcare institutions, implementation partners, donors and the community under a Rotary Global Grant.

The project focuses on Human Milk Bank upgradation, the Human Milk Bank Van, human milk collection support, breastfeeding awareness, identification and registration of potential donor mothers, and community outreach.
