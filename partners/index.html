Together for a Shared Purpose

A collective effort bringing mothers, doctors, healthcare institutions, Rotary organizations, donors and communities together for vulnerable newborns.

Partnership Ecosystem

Four kinds of partnership

  • Rotary partnersClubs and districts that convene, fund and steward the project.
  • Healthcare and institutional partnersInstitutions that hold clinical and operational responsibility.
  • Implementation partnersTeams carrying out day-to-day project work on the ground.
  • Community ecosystemVolunteers, community groups and supporters who make outreach possible.
