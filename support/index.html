Why Support Matters

What support makes possible

The journey of donor human milk requires awareness, safe collection, trained teams, transportation, storage, technology and continuous community outreach. Support helps strengthen this work and extend its reach.

Areas of Impact

Where support is directed

  • Human Milk Bank ecosystemStrengthening the institutional capacity behind safe donation.
  • Community outreachPresence and activity in the communities the project serves.
  • Awareness programmesBreastfeeding and donor milk education for families.
  • Mother engagementGuidance and support for mothers considering donation.
  • Equipment and resourcesThe practical means outreach and collection depend on.
  • TechnologySystems that keep registration, follow-up and records reliable.

Financial Contributions

An enquiry-led approach for now

Bank details, UPI IDs, QR codes and payment links are not published on this website. The official contribution pathway and governance process are being finalized. Until then, please send an enquiry and the appropriate team will respond with approved information.

CSR and Organizational Partnership

Collaboration, shaped together

Rather than fixed packages, collaboration areas are discussed with each organization: outreach, awareness, infrastructure, equipment, technology and sustainability initiatives. What is possible depends on the project's operational capacity at the time.

Transparency and Trust

Only what can be maintained

This initiative publishes what it can verify and commits only to what it can operationally sustain. Grant information, partner recognition and statistics are reviewed before publication and updated as the project progresses.
