# Partner image sources

- `*-page-1.webp`: tightly framed, resized copies of the user-supplied PDF artwork. Only empty transparent margins were removed. Complete original WebP page conversions remain in `../pdf/`.
- `chhanyado.webp`: original logo from https://chhanyado.org/assets/images/logo-new.png, linked on https://chhanyado.org/. Downloaded 2026-09-07; converted losslessly to WebP. Source resolution: 250 × 125.
- `rotary-international.svg`: user-supplied vector of the Rotary International wheel mark (single gold colour, transparent cut-outs), shown in the Partners page hero. Unmodified.
- `rotary-surat-riverside.webp` (host club), `rotary-udhna.webp`, `rotary-tapi.webp`, `rotary-sachin.webp`, `rotary-surat-seaface.webp`, `rotary-shades-valley.webp`, `rotary-district-3060.webp`, `rotary-district-3142.webp`, `rotary-district-6860.webp`: user-supplied "Rotary Club of <name>" / "Rotary District <n>" lockups (Rotary wordmark + wheel + name), generated by the user with an image tool. Supplied on a solid black background; the black was removed here to transparency (brightness-keyed alpha), stray corner sparkles dropped, the two ink colours normalised to the Rotary palette (Royal Blue #17458F, Gold #F7A81B) for a consistent set, then cropped to content and saved as WebP. The three district marks read "Rotary Club of District …" in the supplied artwork; the card labels use the correct "Rotary District …" name.
- `government-medical-college.webp` and `new-civil-hospital.webp`: user-supplied institutional emblems generated with an image tool. Both supplied on a white background, removed here to transparency (the GMC crest is line art, so all near-white went transparent; the combined NCH seal keeps its interior fill, only the outer white was flood-filled), then cropped and saved as WebP. `government-medical-college.webp` is a "Government Medical College Surat" crest; `new-civil-hospital.webp` is a combined "Govt. Medical College / New Civil Hospital, Surat" seal, reused for the paediatrics department as the parent institution's emblem.
- `surat-pediatric-association.webp`, `academy-of-pediatrics-gujarat.webp`, `indian-academy-of-pediatrics.webp`: user-supplied association logos, already on transparent backgrounds. Only cropped to content and converted to WebP; colours and detail unchanged.

Apart from the nine Rotary lockups and the two institutional emblems noted above, no partner artwork was generated with AI. The official-source and association logos retain their supplied resolution; no synthetic details were added.
