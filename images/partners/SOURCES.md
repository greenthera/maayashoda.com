# Partner image sources

- `*-page-1.webp`: tightly framed, resized copies of the user-supplied PDF artwork. Only empty transparent margins were removed. Complete original WebP page conversions remain in `../pdf/`.
- `chhanyado.webp`: original logo from https://chhanyado.org/assets/images/logo-new.png, linked on https://chhanyado.org/. Downloaded 2026-09-07; converted losslessly to WebP. Source resolution: 250 × 125.
- `rotary-international.svg`: user-supplied vector of the Rotary International wheel mark (single gold colour, transparent cut-outs), shown in the Partners page hero. Unmodified.
- `rotary-surat-riverside.webp` (host club), `rotary-udhna.webp`, `rotary-tapi.webp`, `rotary-sachin.webp`, `rotary-surat-seaface.webp`: user-supplied "Rotary Club of <name>" lockups (Rotary wordmark + wheel + club name), generated by the user with an image tool. Supplied on a solid black background; the black was removed here to transparency (brightness-keyed alpha), stray corner sparkles dropped, the two ink colours normalised to the Rotary palette (Royal Blue #17458F, Gold #F7A81B) for a consistent set, then cropped to content and saved as WebP. They replace the earlier `*-page-1.webp` display copies for the host and the four participating clubs.
- `government-medical-college.webp` and `new-civil-hospital.webp`: user-supplied institutional emblems generated with an image tool. Both supplied on a white background, removed here to transparency (the GMC crest is line art, so all near-white went transparent; the combined NCH seal keeps its interior fill, only the outer white was flood-filled), then cropped and saved as WebP. `government-medical-college.webp` is a "Government Medical College Surat" crest; `new-civil-hospital.webp` is a combined "Govt. Medical College / New Civil Hospital, Surat" seal, reused for the paediatrics department as the parent institution's emblem.

Apart from the five Rotary club lockups and the two institutional emblems noted above, no partner artwork was generated with AI. The official-source logos retain their available resolution; no synthetic details were added.
