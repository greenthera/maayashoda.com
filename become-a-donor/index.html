Register Your Interest in Becoming a Milk Donor

If you are interested in learning more about the possibility of milk donation, you can register your interest here. Only basic information is requested, so that the appropriate team can follow up with guidance.

Registering your interest does not automatically confirm donor eligibility.

Who is this for?

You may wish to register if:

  • You are currently breastfeeding and interested in learning more.
  • You believe you may have surplus breast milk.
  • You would like guidance before making any decision.

These are not eligibility rules. They are simply common reasons mothers get in touch.

What happens next?

  1. 1You register your interestA short form, no medical questions.
  2. 2Your registration enters the approved workflowIt reaches the project's communication process.
  3. 3The appropriate team provides guidanceSomeone gets in touch through your preferred channel.
  4. 4Any further process follows the authorized pathwayAssessment, if any, rests with authorized healthcare professionals.

Privacy and data handling

The information you provide is collected to understand your interest and to enable appropriate follow-up through the approved project communication workflow. It is not used to assess eligibility. You can read how information is handled in the Privacy Policy.
