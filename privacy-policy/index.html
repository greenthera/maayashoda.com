1. Introduction

This policy explains how information submitted through the Maa Yashoda website is collected, used and handled. It applies to the public website and the forms published on it.

2. Information We Collect

Through the forms on this website we may collect: name, mobile number, email address, city or area, organization information, enquiry content and information about your interest in milk donation.

The published list will be finalized to match exactly the fields present on the live website.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to respond to enquiries, to communicate about donor interest registration, to support approved next-step communication, to respond to partnership enquiries, to maintain security and to improve website operations.

4. Milk Donor Registration Information

The donor form on this website records interest only. It does not replace an authorized medical, screening or eligibility process, and it is not used to make eligibility decisions.

5. Information Sharing

Information may be shared with authorized project team members, relevant implementation personnel and authorized institutional pathways, and with technology providers acting on the project's behalf. The final list of recipient categories will reflect actual practice.

6. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are applied, including restricted access and secure transmission. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Cookies and Analytics

Analytics and cookie usage will be disclosed here once the final tooling is confirmed. Tracking will not include names, phone numbers, email addresses or health information.

8. Data Retention

Retention periods, or the criteria used to determine them, will be defined by the project before launch and stated here.

9. Rights and Choices

You may request correction of your information, withdraw consent to further communication, or make a data-related request through the contact route published on this website, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

10. Third-Party Services

Categories of third-party services used by the website, such as hosting, form handling and analytics, will be listed here once confirmed.

11. Children's Privacy

The website is intended for adults. Wording here will reflect the actual service and applicable law following legal review.

12. Policy Changes

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

13. Contact

Privacy-related contact information will be published here once approved.
