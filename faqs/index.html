About Maa Yashoda

  • Maa Yashoda is a breastfeeding awareness, human milk donation and Human Milk Bank outreach initiative based in Surat. It works to build awareness, connect interested mothers with the appropriate healthcare pathway, and support the Human Milk Bank through upgradation and community outreach.

  • To make information about breastfeeding and donor human milk available to families, to give mothers who may be able to donate a clear and respectful route to guidance, and to strengthen the ecosystem that makes safe donation possible.

  • Rotary organizations, healthcare and institutional partners, an implementation partner and community stakeholders. The Partners page lists the organizations referenced in the project, subject to final confirmation.

  • The project is supported by Rotary Global Grant GG2694832, which brings host and international Rotary partners together with healthcare institutions and implementation partners around a shared objective.

Becoming a Milk Donor

  • Any mother who would like to learn more about the possibility of milk donation is welcome to register her interest. Registration is a request for information and guidance, not a clinical assessment.

  • No. Registering your interest does not confirm donor eligibility. Eligibility is determined only through the authorized healthcare process, by qualified professionals.

  • Entirely. Milk donation is a voluntary act of generosity, and you may stop at any point in the conversation.

  • No. Many mothers register simply to understand the process. You are free to ask questions and decide later, or not at all.

  • Yes. You can read the About the Project and Milk Bank Van pages, or contact the team with questions before registering.

Registration and Next Steps

  • Your registration enters the approved project communication workflow. The appropriate team may review it and provide further guidance. Any further assessment follows the applicable authorized pathway.

  • Only basic details: your name, mobile number, city or area, and what kind of guidance you are looking for. The public form does not ask for medical history.

  • Through the contact method you choose on the form, from among the channels the team actively monitors.

  • Yes. The Contact page provides a general enquiry route for questions before registration.

Understanding the Process

  • Donor human milk is breast milk provided by a screened donor mother through an authorized Human Milk Bank process, for use where it is clinically appropriate. Detailed clinical wording for this answer is pending healthcare approval.

  • The process is defined and operated by the authorized Human Milk Bank healthcare team. A public description of the steps will be published once approved by the relevant healthcare authorities.

  • Eligibility is determined by authorized healthcare professionals according to institutional protocols. This website does not assess or decide eligibility.

  • Donated milk is handled according to the approved Human Milk Bank protocols of the relevant healthcare institution. Detailed handling information is pending healthcare approval.

Human Milk Bank

  • A Human Milk Bank is a healthcare facility that collects, screens, processes and stores donated human milk for use where clinically appropriate, operating under institutional protocols.

  • The Human Milk Bank is the healthcare and institutional centre of the initiative. Awareness and outreach activity exists to support it, not to substitute for it.

  • Authorized healthcare professionals at the relevant institution. The project's awareness and outreach team does not perform clinical activities.

Human Milk Bank Van

  • The Human Milk Bank Van is the outreach component of the initiative. It carries awareness, information and guidance into communities and connects interested mothers with the appropriate pathway.

  • Because distance and unfamiliarity keep willing mothers from ever reaching a Human Milk Bank. Outreach closes that gap by arriving where mothers already are.

  • No. The Van supports outreach and connection. Clinical processes remain with the Human Milk Bank and its authorized healthcare team.

  • Community awareness leads to interest, interest leads to appropriate guidance, and guidance connects a mother to the authorized process where applicable.

Supporting the Initiative

  • Through awareness, community collaboration or approved contribution opportunities. The Support the Cause page describes the available routes and provides an enquiry form.

  • Yes. Send a support enquiry describing your area of interest and the relevant team will respond regarding appropriate opportunities.

  • Partnership is possible across outreach, awareness, infrastructure, equipment, technology and sustainability initiatives, subject to project capacity and approval.

  • Use the general enquiry form on the Contact page. Approved and actively monitored contact channels are published there.
