Human Milk Bank Van

Taking Awareness and Connection Closer to Communities

The Human Milk Bank Van is an outreach component of the broader initiative. It carries information, guidance and human presence into communities, supporting the Human Milk Bank rather than replacing any part of it.

Why the Van Matters

Information rarely travels on its own

“When a mother is willing to share her gift of nourishment, distance should not become a barrier.”

Awareness campaigns reach the families who already read, search and attend. The mothers who most need a conversation about milk donation are often the ones least likely to encounter it.

A physical presence changes that. When outreach arrives in a neighbourhood, questions get asked out loud, families join the conversation, and hesitation has somewhere to go.

Taking Outreach Closer

What mobility makes possible

  • PresenceAwareness activity in the places where mothers and families already gather.
  • ConversationSpace to ask questions without an appointment or a hospital visit.
  • ContinuityA recognizable point of contact that returns, rather than a one-time campaign.
  • ConnectionA route from community interest to the appropriate healthcare pathway.

Service areas, routes and schedules will be published only once operationally confirmed.

The Van's Role

Two distinct components

01Human Milk BankThe healthcare and institutional component. Clinical processes, screening, handling and eligibility decisions sit here, under authorized healthcare professionals.
02Human Milk Bank VanThe outreach and connection component. Awareness, guidance, registration support and community engagement, as described by the approved project model.

The Van does not independently perform clinical activities.
