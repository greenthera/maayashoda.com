1. Our Intention

This website aims to be usable by as many visitors as reasonably possible, including people using assistive technology, keyboard navigation or smaller screens.

2. What We Work On

Keyboard navigation, screen-reader compatibility, clear document structure, adequate colour contrast, responsive layouts, readable typography and accessible forms.

3. Known Limitations

Some content awaits approval and may be updated. Where limitations are identified, they will be listed here along with planned improvements.

4. Reporting an Issue

If you encounter a barrier on this website, please tell us through the contact route published on this website and we will look into it. An approved accessibility contact will be published before launch.
