About Maa Yashoda

Maa Yashoda is a breastfeeding awareness, human milk donation and Human Milk Bank outreach initiative based in Surat, supported by Rotary organizations, healthcare institutions, implementation partners and the community.

Project title image — outreach, van or milk bank photograph
The Story Behind the Initiative

It began with a gap in awareness

Human Milk Banks have existed within neonatal care for decades, yet most families have never heard of them. Mothers with surplus milk rarely know that a safe, guided pathway exists through which their generosity could support another family's newborn.

The initiative was formed around that gap. Rather than beginning with infrastructure alone, it begins with awareness, with conversation, and with the trust required for a mother to consider donation at all.

Alongside awareness, the project supports the upgradation of the Human Milk Bank and the introduction of a Human Milk Bank Van, so that outreach and institutional capacity grow together.

The Challenge

What stands in the way

  • Limited awarenessBreastfeeding support and donor human milk remain unfamiliar subjects for many families.
  • DistanceMothers willing to help may not be able to travel to a Human Milk Bank.
  • UncertaintyWithout clear information, mothers hesitate, not because they are unwilling, but because no one has explained the process.
  • Fragmented connectionInterest expressed in a community rarely reaches the institution that can act on it.
The Maa Yashoda Approach

Five connected pillars

  • 01AwarenessClear, respectful information about breastfeeding and donor human milk.
  • 02Donor engagementA simple way for interested mothers to register and be guided.
  • 03Human Milk Bank ecosystemSupport for the institutional capacity that makes donation possible.
  • 04Community outreachPresence in neighbourhoods, not only in hospitals.
  • 05Human Milk Bank VanMobility that carries awareness and connection closer to mothers.
Project Objectives

What the project sets out to do

Objectives are drawn from the project brief and remain subject to stakeholder review.

  1. 01Strengthen and upgrade the Human Milk Bank.
  2. 02Introduce a Human Milk Bank Van for community outreach.
  3. 03Support safe and organized human milk collection.
  4. 04Build breastfeeding awareness among families.
  5. 05Identify and register potential donor mothers.
  6. 06Strengthen newborn and neonatal nutrition support.
  7. 07Extend outreach into communities across the project area.

The Ecosystem

No single organization does this alone

Meet Our Partners

The initiative works through four groups: Rotary organizations that convene and fund the project, healthcare institutions that hold clinical responsibility, implementation partners who carry out day-to-day work, and community stakeholders who make outreach possible.

  • Rotary partnersClubs and districts that convene, fund and steward the project.
  • Healthcare and institutional partnersInstitutions that hold clinical and operational responsibility.
  • Implementation partnersTeams carrying out day-to-day project work on the ground.
  • Community ecosystemVolunteers, community groups and supporters who make outreach possible.
The Bigger Vision

A model that can be repeated

The intention is to build something that continues after the grant period: an awareness culture around breastfeeding, a working connection between communities and the Human Milk Bank, and an outreach approach that other cities could adapt. Growth will follow what can be operationally sustained.
